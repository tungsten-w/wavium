# P5-1: Daily digest ("what needs a reply or an action today")

- Branch: `night/P5-1` (on top of `night/P2-4`) · Instance: session A (tmux session "main") · Date: 2026-11-13
- Status: done

## Done

- **Module** `server/agent/digest/` (one line in `server/agent/index.ts`; the
  AI module instance is now shared with it):
  - `build.ts`: the digest from four injected sources:
    - **mail** of the last 7 days in the inbox, curated (no ads, spam,
      newsletters, social), that needs a reply (not answered yet) or an action,
      never suspicious mail (≥ 0.6, the Mail chip threshold); sorted by urgency,
      then deadline, then unread first; max 15, each with a link that opens it
      in Mail;
    - today's **events** (all-day first, cancelled left out);
    - open **tasks** due today or overdue;
    - today's **Daily Todo** items (top-level, not done, not the events and
      tasks already listed);
    - a **headline** ("1 mail needs a reply, 1 needs an action · 3 events, the
      first at 09:30 · 2 tasks due (1 overdue)") and a plain-text version for
      notifications.

    "Today" is the user's day in their Nextcloud time zone (DST-safe). A source
    that fails is left out and named; the rest is still built.
  - `index.ts`:
    - store (own tables `atrium_digest_settings`, `atrium_digest`, 14 days kept);
    - the **morning run** (every minute; once per local day at the user's time;
      opt-in);
    - **ntfy notification** (opt-in; uses the instance `ATRIUM_NTFY_URL`), with a
      link to the Dashboard;
    - optional **AI summary**: one or two sentences in the user's language, task
      `summary` through Settings > AI and the budget, off by default, never
      blocking. It is written in the morning or on refresh, never on a plain
      GET.
  - Owner routes:
    - `GET /agent/v1/digest`;
    - `POST /agent/v1/digest/refresh`;
    - `GET` / `PUT /agent/v1/digest/settings` (session; `OCS-APIRequest` on
      writes).

    Turning it on after today's time starts tomorrow (no surprise notification).
- `server/agent/ai.ts`: `forTask(uid, task)` gives other modules the user's
  provider for a task, model chosen (P2-5), behind the budget (P2-3), so they
  never import `server/ai` themselves.
- **UI** `apps/dashboard/web/Today.tsx`:
  - a **Today** card at the top of the Dashboard: the AI summary, the headline,
    then mail (Reply / Act, now / today marks), calendar, tasks (overdue) and
    today's list, each linking into its app;
  - refresh, and a note when a source could not be read;
  - a settings dialog: every morning on/off, time, notification, AI summary.
