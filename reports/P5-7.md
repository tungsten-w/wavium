## Done

- `server/agent/extract/` (`extractModule(ai)`):
  - the user's AI (task `extract`, through Settings > AI and the budget) reads
    one mail and proposes events and tasks (forced tool);
  - times come back in the user's local time and Atrium adds the offset of
    their Nextcloud time zone (DST-safe);
  - the answer is checked: invalid dates dropped, items from before the mail
    dropped, end defaults (1 h, or the next day for all-day), at most 5 of
    each.
- Owner routes:
  - `POST /agent/v1/extract {db_id | message_key}` → proposals;
  - `POST /agent/v1/extract/create {kind, item, db_id}` → the event or task is
    created through the calendar app (P3-2), with a note "From the mail …".
- Agent tool `mail_extract_dates` (read): the proposals. Agents create them
  with the calendar tools under their own grants.
- Mail reader: More actions → "Find dates…" opens a dialog with the proposals;
  each one is created only on its own click ("Add to calendar" / "Add task").
